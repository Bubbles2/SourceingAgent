How it works

Four steps from catalogue to hammer

1

Discover auctions

Browse upcoming sales by date, auction house and location, or open the monthly calendar.

2

Read the catalogue

Each auction links to the house's official PDF catalogue so you can study every lot.

3

Request a bid

Pick the auction and catalogue, enter the lot number and your maximum bid in EUR, add instructions.

4

The agent bids for you

Once accepted, your request reserves the lot. The agent bids in the room and reports the result.
